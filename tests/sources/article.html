How we verify cited sources before publishing

Every research report we ship goes through a link audit. Our team measured the redirect chain on every cited link, recorded the final URL, and compared the page text with the quoted excerpt.

We found that roughly one link in eight pointed at a page that no longer carried the claim, usually because the publisher had restructured its archive.

The fix was boring: a script that fetches the raw HTML, follows redirects and searches for the quote. It runs in seconds and costs nothing.
